💡 How The Algorithm Works (Uniform Coverage Mode)

First priority: Hit your target ratio. The algorithm only considers gear sets that can achieve your target (within 0.5% tolerance).

1

2
Try adding each remaining gear: Test what happens if we add each available gear to our set
3
Filter by target: Only keep sets that can hit your target ratio (or just above it)
4
Score by uniformity: Among sets that hit target, pick the one with most even spacing
5
Repeat: Keep adding gears, always prioritizing target-hitting sets with best uniformity

Result: You get gear sets that CAN hit your target ratio AND have the most uniform coverage from 1:1 to target!
